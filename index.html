For Jacob and Damo

Japan

Ten days in Tokyo or Osaka. Late January 2027 first, later in the year if that suits, and Shanghai as the wildcard.

Jacob from Brisbane, Damo from Melbourne. Prices from Google Flights on 11 Oct 2026, return economy per person in AUD. They will move.

The short version

Leave Australia Day

26 January came out cheapest across the board. Fly into Tokyo, each from your own city, and catch the shinkansen to Osaka during the trip.

  • Cheapest, still sensible $2,949for both Jetstar, leave Tue 26 Jan, back Fri 5 Feb

    Jacob non-stop Brisbane to Narita, $1,516. Damo via Cairns, $1,433. Bags are extra on Jetstar, roughly $100 to $200 return each.

  • Most comfortable ~$4,800for both Both on non-stops

    Jacob on Qantas from Brisbane, $2,322. Damo on Qantas or JAL from Melbourne, about $2,100 to $2,500. Bags and meals in, about nine to ten hours.

  • Fly together ~$2,800for both Meet in Cairns

    Jacob leaves Brisbane 6:40am ($1,336 to $1,381), Damo leaves Melbourne 6:15am ($1,433 to $1,525). Both land at Narita at 6:10pm, so most likely the same plane from Cairns. Check the flight numbers before booking.

Later in 2027

Cheaper if you can wait

The same trip drops to about $1,000 to $1,300 each from March. These skip late April and Japan's Golden Week (29 April to 5 May), when prices spike.

  • Best weather $2,405for both Late May, Tue 18 to Fri 28 May

    Jacob on Jetstar non-stop, $1,108. Damo on Cathay via Hong Kong, $1,297, or Qantas non-stop for $1,968. Warm, dry and past the holiday crowds.

  • Cheapest month $2,585both non-stop Early June, Tue 8 to Fri 18 June

    Jacob on Jetstar non-stop, $1,006. Damo on Qantas non-stop, $1,579, the cheapest Qantas fare we found all year. The catch is the start of Tokyo's rainy season.

  • Early blossom $2,315for both Mid March, Tue 16 to Fri 26 March

    Jacob on Jetstar non-stop, $1,265. Damo on Jetstar via Cairns, $1,050. Cherry blossom usually starts in Tokyo in the last week of March, so a few days later catches more of it and costs more.

September onwards isn't on sale yet. Airlines open about 11 months out, so check again in the new year.

China instead?

Shanghai for less

$878Jacob, Brisbane to Shanghai, May 2027

China is the cheapest option we found. In late May, Jacob flies China Southern via Guangzhou for $878 and Damo flies Cathay via Hong Kong for $1,003. That's $1,881 for both, about $500 under Japan the same week. Late January is $1,261 and $1,077.

  • The visa is the catch. Australians get 30 days visa free, but it's only confirmed to 31 December 2026. China has extended it every November so far. Check before booking anything for 2027.
  • Late January runs into the Chinese New Year rush (New Year's Day is Sat 6 February 2027), so trains and hotels are packed. May is the better China trip.
  • Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay with your own card before you leave, and get a travel eSIM so Google and Instagram still work.
  • There are non-stops (China Eastern from Brisbane, several airlines from Melbourne), but they didn't show at a good price in these searches.
